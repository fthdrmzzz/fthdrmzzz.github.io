I am a security enthusiast exploring operating system security through capability-based access control. In Fall 2025, I will be joining the team of [Sven Bugiel](https://svenbugiel.de/) at [CISPA](https://cispa.de/en) to dive deeper in this area.

Previously, I completed my master's in the [Erasmus Mundus Cyberus Joint Master](https://master-cyberus.eu/) program, specializing in the IoT Security track. My thesis at [IMDEA Software Institute](https://software.imdea.org/) named [Enhancing System Security Through Static Analysis by Detecting Dormant Vulnerabilities](https://difusion.ulb.ac.be/vufind/Record/ULB-MASTER:oai:dipot.ulb.ac.be:2013/377489/Holdings) focuses on identifying unreachable vulnerabilities in Debian-based systems to enable more effective vulnerability prioritization.

My interest in security began during my undergraduate studies at [Sabancı University](https://www.sabanciuniv.edu/en), where I worked on [detecting timing-based side-channel attacks](https://silm-workshop.github.io/2023-papers/silm2023-timeinspector.pdf) for my bachelor’s thesis.
